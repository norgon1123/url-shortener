We need the shortener to be more reliable, and we want analytics.

Reliability first. It has fallen over twice this quarter and both times we heard
about it from a customer rather than from our own monitoring, which is the part
that actually embarrassed us. Whatever you do here, we should know before they
do. It also needs to cope better when things around it are unhealthy — the last
outage started somewhere else and we made it worse.

On analytics: the per-link numbers we already report are not enough. People want
to understand how their links are performing overall, not one at a time, and
sales has been asked for this by three of our larger accounts. One of them
wanted to know whether we could break it down by where the clicks came from.

This is the next thing we want after the sign-up work, so treat it as the
current priority. We would like it soon.

Constraints are as before: Java 21 and Spring Boot, PostgreSQL and Redis, no new
database technology, no UI in this build.
